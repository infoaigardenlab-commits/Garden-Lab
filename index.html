Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about using our AI tools.

Is AI Garden Lab really free?

Yes, AI Garden Lab is completely free for all gardeners. Our mission is to make expert gardening information available to everyone. We want to encourage sustainable and healthy gardening practices.

Can I use this on my mobile phone?

Absolutely! AI Garden Lab works great on mobile devices. You can take pictures of your plants with your phone's camera and get immediate analysis right in your garden.

Do I need to sign up?

No sign-up is needed to use our diagnostic tools. We want you to get the help you need right away without any obstacles.

How do the AI tools work?

Our AI tools use powerful machine learning models trained on large sets of plant images. When you upload a photo, the AI examines it to find patterns and offer scientific recommendations.

How accurate are the results?

Our AI is very accurate and always getting better. However, for important plant health issues, we recommend using our analysis alongside professional horticultural advice.
